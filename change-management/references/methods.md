# Change methods — how this plugin describes and cites them

The plugin is method-neutral. It explains each framework in its own words and
links to the owner. It does not reproduce proprietary instruments, wording,
templates or scoring sheets. See [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md) and
[ATTRIBUTION.md](../ATTRIBUTION.md) in the plugin root.

Read the practice profile's `method` field first. Use that framework's
vocabulary where it helps the user; otherwise use the plain-English terms in
the right-hand column.

## Using a framework in an artefact

- Name it once with attribution, e.g. "Barrier diagnosis is ADKAR-informed
  (ADKAR® is a registered trademark of Prosci, Inc.)."
- Do not copy question wording, scoring scales or templates from the owner.
  The plugin's own scales live in [rating-scales.md](rating-scales.md).
- If the organisation holds a licence and has its own copies of the official
  instruments, the user can paste them in; use them for that organisation's
  work only and do not save them into the plugin.

## Evidence the plugin may cite

Only cite figures with a named source and edition. Two the research checked:

- Prosci *Best Practices in Change Management*: active and visible sponsorship
  is the top contributor to success in every edition of the study.
- Prosci's change-portfolio article reports 78% of respondents nearing, at or
  past change saturation; later editions report about 73%. Name the edition.

Vendor-reported adoption or ROI figures are not independent benchmarks — do
not use them in practitioner-facing output.
