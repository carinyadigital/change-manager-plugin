# Sustainability check

Ten questions, written for this plugin, to judge whether a change will last
once the project team steps back. Rate each from evidence:

- **3 — Strong:** clear evidence the factor is in place.
- **2 — Partial:** some evidence, or in place for some groups only.
- **1 — Weak:** no evidence, or evidence that it is missing.

No evidence means 1 or 2, never 3. Any factor at 1 is a priority for the
reinforcement plan. Rate per group where groups differ.

| # | Factor | Question | Evidence to look for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Visible benefit | Can the people doing the work see that the new way is better for them or their customers? | Feedback, adoption review comments, before/after measures |
| 2 | Easier than the old way | Is the new way at least as quick and simple as what it replaced, day to day? | Task times, help-desk themes, workarounds reported |
| 3 | Old way removed | Have the old system, form, report or approval path been switched off or locked? | Decommission dates, access lists |
| 4 | Leaders keep asking | Do team leaders and senior leaders check and talk about the new way in their normal routines? | Meeting agendas, leader check-ins, sponsor plan actions |
| 5 | Built into the job | Do goals, role descriptions, procedures and induction reflect the new way? | Updated documents, induction materials |
| 6 | Measured and seen | Is someone outside the project watching the key measures, and do teams see the results? | BAU dashboard, named measure owner |
| 7 | Capability stays | Can new starters and movers learn the new way without the project team? | Job aids location, induction, trainers in BAU |
| 8 | Support stays | Is there a known, lasting place to get help after hypercare ends? | Support model, champion network continuing |
| 9 | People involved | Have the people affected had a say in fixing what wasn't working after go-live? | Feedback loop records, changes made |
| 10 | Fits what's next | Is the change protected from being undone by upcoming changes or restructures? | Portfolio heatmap, upcoming initiatives |

healthcare improvement will last; it is published by NHS England and appears
to be under the Open Government Licence (verify per document before reuse).
The questions above are this plugin's own and do not reproduce it. See
