## Bundling adapted material later

If a future release adapts text from a CC BY or OGL source, the adapted file
must:

1. carry a header stating the source, licence, and what was changed;
2. be listed in the table above with "Adapted" in the "How used" column;
3. use the attribution wording the licensor requests (for APS: "Based on the
   Australian Government Department of Home Affairs APS Change Framework"),
   with no implied endorsement and no government logos.

See the release gate in [docs/ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/carinyadigital/change-manager-plugin/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md).
