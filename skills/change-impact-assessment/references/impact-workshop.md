# Running an impact assessment

Original guidance written for this plugin. Read it when planning how to
gather impacts — a workshop, interviews, or a desk review — and when a
dimension needs probing. Scores use [rating-scales.md](rating-scales.md).

## Choose the way in

| Situation | Best way to gather impacts | Who to involve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solution and process design are documented | Desk review first, then a 60–90 minute validation workshop per group | Business process owner, two or three people who do the work today, a team leader |
| Design still moving | Short interviews with process owners now; workshop once design settles | Process owner, solution lead |
| Restructure or role change | Small, confidential working group; HR present; no open workshop until consultation allows | Business owner, HR / workforce partner, employee relations if required |
| Many locations or shifts | One workshop per location or shift that works differently; do not assume one group's answer holds for another | Local team leaders and frontline staff |

Always include at least one person who does the work today. Designers and
managers routinely miss handoffs, workarounds and the reports people rely on.

## Before the session

1. Pull the current-state and future-state material: process maps, solution
   design, policy drafts, org charts, the business case.
2. Draft a first pass of today → after for each group, marked as draft.
3. List the questions only the business can answer.
4. Confirm whether anything is confidential (role changes, location changes)
   and who may see the register.

## Workshop outline (60–90 minutes per group)

| Time | Step | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Why we're here; what is decided and what is open; how the register is used | Shared purpose |
| 15 min | Walk through a day, week and month in the life of this group today | Current-state list |
| 25 min | For each dimension below, ask the prompt questions; record today → after | Draft impact rows |
| 15 min | Score each changed dimension 0–4 together; capture the reason for any 3 or 4 | Scores with evidence |
| 10 min | What support would this group need? What is the worst week to land it? | Support needs, timing constraints |
| 5 min | What happens next; when they will see the draft | Agreed review date |

Score together, then sense-check across groups afterwards — workshop groups
tend to rate their own change higher than neighbouring groups do.

## Dimension prompts

Ask only the prompts that apply. Skip a dimension that does not change.

| Dimension | Prompt questions |
| --- | --- |
| Process | Which steps start, stop or change order? Who approves what now? Which handoffs to other teams change? What exceptions or workarounds exist today, and will they still work? |
| Systems and tools | Which screens, systems, forms or spreadsheets change? What do people stop using? What data do they enter or look up differently? Is any manual step added during transition? |
| Roles and responsibilities | Which decisions move up, down or sideways? Which tasks move to or from another team? Does anyone's job description need to change? |
| Skills | What will people need to know or be able to do that they cannot today? How long does it take to get proficient? Are any current skills no longer needed? |
| Behaviours and culture | What do people need to do differently in how they work together, share information or make decisions? What will be rewarded or noticed that wasn't before? |
| Structure and reporting lines | Do reporting lines, team membership or team size change? Is anyone's manager changing? |
| Location and working pattern | Does where, when or how often people work change — site, shifts, rosters, travel, hybrid pattern? |
| Data and reporting | Which reports, dashboards or measures do people rely on today? What replaces them, and when? Do targets change? |
| Customer interaction | What will customers see, hear or be asked to do differently? Which customer questions will staff now need to answer? |
| Policies and controls | Which policies, delegations, compliance checks or audit steps change? |

## Timing and duration

For each group, also capture:

- **Starts / ends** — the months the group is actively asked to do something
  (briefings, training, go-live, hypercare). These feed the portfolio heatmap.
- **One-off or lasting** — a one-off event (a move, a cutover weekend) is
  rated differently from a lasting change in daily work. Note it in the
  evidence column.

## Sense checks before writing

- Every 3 or 4 has a reason someone outside the workshop would accept.
- Managers appear as their own group, not hidden inside their teams.
- Groups that experience the change differently are split.
- "Not changing" is written down — people need to hear it.
- Anything heard in confidence is either excluded or marked for the owner's
  review before the register is shared.
