# Sizing the change — worksheet

Original worksheet written for this plugin. It turns the signals in
[framework-selector.md](framework-selector.md) into a repeatable size, so two
people sizing the same change land in the same place. Score each signal from
evidence in the record; leave it blank if there is none and say so.

## Score each signal

| # | Signal | 1 — smaller | 2 — medium | 3 — larger | Evidence from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Highest group headline impact | 1–2 | 3 | 4 | `impact-register.md` |
| 2 | Groups at high priority | One | Two or three | Four or more, or one very large group | `score_impacts.ts` output |
| 3 | People affected | Under 50 | 50–500 | Over 500, or several sites | impact register, `~~hris` |
| 4 | Roles redesigned, removed or created | None | Some changed duties | Roles removed or created | impact register |
| 5 | How different the new way is | Familiar tasks, new tool | New process and tool | New ways of working, decisions or behaviours | impact register dimensions |
| 6 | Sponsor and health check | Committed; Healthy | Some concern | Gaps; Material concern | `stakeholder-register.md`, `pct-assessment.md` |
| 7 | Organisational readiness | Ready to proceed | Mixed readiness | Low readiness | `org-readiness.md` |
| 8 | Competing change for the same groups | Few | Watch cells | Saturated cells | `portfolio-heatmap.md` |
| 9 | Timeline | Comfortable | Tight | Very tight or fixed by regulation | `scope.md` |
| 10 | Employee relations | No consultation needed | Some consultation | Formal consultation; industrial sensitivity | HR, `risk-register.md` |

## Read the score

Average the signals you could score (at least six; otherwise report the size
as provisional).

| Average | Size | What it usually means for effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1.7 | Smaller | Briefings, a job aid and a support route; light-touch readiness check; short reinforcement |
| 1.7 to 2.3 | Medium | Manager toolkit; targeted training; a small champion group for the most affected teams; readiness checks before go-live; 90-day adoption review |
| Over 2.3 | Larger | Sponsor roadmap and coalition; full champion network; structured training plan; regular pulse; readiness checks on cadence; go/no-go gate; 180-day reinforcement |

**Overrides.** Any signal at 3 for signals 1, 4 or 10 makes the change at
least medium, whatever the average. Record the scores and the override in the
strategy's "Size of the change" table — the reasoning matters more than the
label.

These bands are planning defaults written for this plugin, not research
findings. The practice profile may set its own.
