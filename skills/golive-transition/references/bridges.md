# Bridges Transition Model — cheat-sheet

The Bridges Transition Model belongs to William Bridges Associates. Described
here in our own words. Further reading:
https://wmbridges.com/about/what-is-transition/

## The idea

*Change* is the external event (the new system goes live on a date).
*Transition* is the internal adjustment people make, and it runs on its own
clock:

| Phase | What people experience | What helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ending** | Letting go of something — a skill, status, team, routine, identity | Name what is ending; acknowledge losses honestly; mark the ending |
| **Neutral zone** | The in-between: uncertainty, lower productivity, also creativity | Temporary structures, frequent contact, short-term goals, room to try things |
| **New beginning** | Commitment to the new way and a new identity in it | Clear purpose, a picture of the future, a part to play, early success |

## When it fits

Changes with real loss — restructures, role changes, mergers, relocations,
retiring a well-loved system — where the emotional side will decide adoption.

## How the plugin uses it

- `resistance-management` — the "what's ending, for whom" lens alongside the
  barrier diagnostic; loss is not the same as resistance.
- `communications-plan` / `comms-draft` — acknowledge endings before selling
  the new beginning.
- `golive-transition` — support for the neutral zone during hypercare.
