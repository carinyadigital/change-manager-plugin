# Readiness criteria — what counts as evidence

Original guidance written for this plugin. For each default criterion in
[rating-scales.md](rating-scales.md), what makes it Green, what keeps it
Amber, and where to look. Use the organisation's own criteria if the
practice profile sets them.

| Criterion | Green needs | Stays Amber when | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understands why | Pulse or briefing feedback shows most of the group can explain the reason (e.g. pulse P-C1 agreement), from at least the minimum group size | Only that messages were sent or briefings held | `~~survey`, `docs/change/pulse/`, champion feedback |
| Knows what changes for them | Group-specific briefing held and questions answered; pulse "I know what will be different" agreement | Only a general all-staff message | `comms/` drafts sent, briefing attendance, pulse |
| Leader has briefed them | The group's leader has briefed **their own team**, with date | The leader attended a briefing but has not cascaded | `sponsor-plan.md`, leader confirmation, champion feedback |
| Trained | Completion **and** a practice or assessment result for the tasks in the TNA | Completion only, or catch-up sessions still to run | `~~lms`, `tna.md`, trainer sign-off |
| Job aids available | Job aids reviewed by an SME and published where people work | Drafts exist but are not published, or not SME-reviewed | `qrg/`, intranet location |
| Access and equipment ready | Accounts provisioned and tested by users; equipment in place | Requests raised but not confirmed, or tested only by IT | `~~itsm`, `~~project tracker`, user test log |
| Support route known | Support route published and pulse "I know where to go" agreement | Route agreed but not yet publicised | `golive-plan.md`, FAQ, pulse |
| Confidence (self-reported) | Pulse "I feel confident I will be able to work the new way" agreement at an agreed level | Below the agreed level, or not yet asked | `~~survey`, `docs/change/pulse/` |

## Confidence check questions

If no pulse is running, ask these four questions from the pulse question bank
(`stakeholder-pulse`), word for word, so results stay comparable with any
later pulse. Ask the whole group, report % agree in aggregate only, and do not
treat them as evidence of ability:

1. **P-C2** — I know what will be different for me and my team.
2. **P-M1** — The training I have had (or have booked) covers what I need for my actual work.
3. **P-C5** — I know where to go with questions or problems about this change.
4. **P-C6** — I feel confident I will be able to work the new way when it starts.

Use the pulse bank's agreement scale and reporting rules.

## Evidence quality

| Strongest | | Weakest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed or measured (assessment results, access test logs, system data) | Reported by the group (pulse, champion feedback) | Asserted by the project ("they're ready") |

Asserted evidence keeps a criterion Amber. Record who asserted it and when.
