## Expectations checklist

### Primary sponsor

- [ ] Explains why the change is happening and why now, in person
- [ ] Makes or unblocks decisions on time
- [ ] Secures the people, money and time the change needs
- [ ] Is visibly present at key moments in every phase
- [ ] Holds their direct reports to account for leading the change in their area
- [ ] Hears feedback from the change team and frontline, and acts on it
- [ ] Stays involved after go-live until the change is embedded

### Leaders down the line

- [ ] Understand the change and what it means for their own role first
- [ ] Brief their teams on what changes for them, and what does not
- [ ] Answer questions honestly, including "we don't know yet"
- [ ] Notice and raise resistance and support needs early
- [ ] Release people for training and engagement activities
- [ ] Recognise and reinforce the new way of working after go-live
