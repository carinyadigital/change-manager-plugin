## Watch for

| Signal | Means | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talking about the old way as better, nostalgia | Ending not yet acknowledged | Name it; give space; don't argue |
| Confusion, low output, frustration well after go-live | Stuck in the in-between | More contact, clearer short-term goals, check targets |
| Using the new way, starting to suggest improvements | New beginning | Recognise it; share the story (`reinforcement`) |
