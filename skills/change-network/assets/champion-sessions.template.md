# Champion sessions — <initiative>

## Onboarding session (60–90 minutes)

**When:** <date, before teams are briefed> · **Who:** all champions,
change lead, a sponsor or senior leader for the opening

| Time | Item | Led by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Why this change, why now — and why we need you | Sponsor or senior leader | Use the change story |
| 15 min | What changes for each group, and what doesn't | Change lead | From the impact register |
| 10 min | The champion role: do, don't, time, length | Change lead | Hand out the role description |
| 15 min | Hard questions — what will people ask you, and what can we say? | All | Capture questions for the FAQ |
| 10 min | Your toolkit and how to escalate | Change lead | Where it lives; who answers |
| 10 min | How feedback works and how you'll hear back | Change lead | Cadence; feedback form; "you said, we did" |
| 5 min | Next steps and dates | Change lead | Next huddle; training dates |

**Before the session:** toolkit drafts ready; FAQ v1; the change story
agreed; managers have agreed release time.

**After the session:** send the toolkit link, the date of the next huddle,
and answers to questions that couldn't be answered on the day.

## Regular huddle (30 minutes)

**Cadence:** <fortnightly in Prepare, weekly in the six weeks before go-live,
then fortnightly, then monthly> · **Format:** <in person / online>

| Time | Item | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | What's new: decisions made, dates, anything that changed | Champions hear before their teams |
| 15 min | Round the room: what are people saying in your team? | Use the rotating prompts below |
| 5 min | Answers to last time's questions — "you said, we did" | Close every loop |
| 5 min | What we need from you before next time | One or two asks, not ten |

**Rotating prompts** (pick two each time):

- What are people saying about the change — the good and the bad?
- What questions came up that you couldn't answer?
- Is anyone working around the new way? What makes the old way easier?
- What has your manager done or said about the change recently?
- What one thing should we fix or explain next?

**After every huddle:** log feedback in `network-feedback.csv` by group and
theme (never by person); confirm who owns each open item.

## Closing session (30 minutes, at the end of the role)

- Thank champions, with the sponsor present
- What worked, what didn't — feeds `lessons-learned`
- What continues in business as usual, and who looks after it — feeds
  `bau-handover`
