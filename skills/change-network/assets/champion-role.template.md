## What a champion does

- Shares what is changing and why with their team, in their own words
- Shows colleagues how the new way works, once trained
- Collects questions and concerns and passes them to the change team
- Spots problems early — confusion, workarounds, things that don't work
- Helps colleagues in the first weeks after go-live
- Tells the change team what is working, so it can be shared

## What a champion does not do

- Deliver news about roles, pay, hours or job security — that is the
  manager's job, with HR
- Act as the help desk — technical problems go to <support route>
- Replace their manager in leading the team through the change
- Speak for the project in public or to customers

## What we offer champions

- Early briefings, before their teams hear
- Training ahead of their teams, and practice access
- A toolkit: briefing notes, FAQ, demo, escalation route
- A direct line to the change team, and answers back to the questions they
  raise
- Recognition of the role <how — e.g. noted in development conversations>
