# Reinforcement mechanisms — by weak factor

Original catalogue written for this plugin. For each factor in the
[sustainability check](sustainability-check.md) rated 1 or 2, the mechanisms
that usually help, who owns them, and what goes wrong. Pick two or three that
fit the evidence; a long list of mechanisms nobody owns does not reinforce
anything.

| Weak factor | Mechanisms that usually help | Typical owner | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Visible benefit | Share measured before/after results with the teams doing the work; one real story per group; show customer feedback | Business owner, comms | Claims without data; generic "success" messages |
| 2. Easier than the old way | Fix the top two friction points from the help-desk themes; simplify a step; update the job aid; ask the group what still slows them | Project or process owner | Treating friction as resistance |
| 3. Old way removed | Switch off, lock or archive the old system, form, report or approval path; set a date and say it in advance; remove shadow spreadsheets with the team, not around them | Business owner, IT | Switching off before the new way works; no date |
| 4. Leaders keep asking | Add the new way to team meeting agendas, one-to-ones and leader dashboards; sponsor asks about it at a visible moment each month | People leaders, sponsor | Attention moving to the next project |
| 5. Built into the job | Update role descriptions, procedures, induction, delegations and performance goals | HR, process owner | Old procedures still in the policy library |
| 6. Measured and seen | Name a BAU owner for the key measure; show results to teams monthly; act on the threshold in the measurement plan | Business owner | A dashboard nobody looks at |
| 7. Capability stays | Job aids where people work; new-starter induction covers the new way; trainers or super-users in BAU | Training lead, team leaders | Knowledge leaving with the project team |
| 8. Support stays | Publish the lasting support route; keep a champion or super-user per team for a set period; hand over to the service desk with known issues | Service owner, change network | Support stopping at hypercare exit with no notice |
| 9. People involved | Run a "what still isn't working" session per group; fix one thing and tell them ("you said, we did") | Change lead, team leaders | Asking for feedback and going quiet |
| 10. Fits what's next | Check the portfolio heatmap for changes that undo or collide with this one; brief the next initiative's team on what must be kept | Portfolio or PMO lead | A restructure that reverses the new routine |

## Recognition that works

- **Specific:** names the behaviour ("approving refunds in the new screen
  first time"), not just "great work".
- **Timely:** close to when it happened.
- **From someone who matters to the person:** usually their manager or a
  senior leader they respect.
- **Fair:** a team or group by default; individuals only with their
  agreement and where it will not feel like being singled out.

## The reinforcement calendar

| When after go-live | Typical focus |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–4 | Fix friction; quick wins shared; old way's switch-off date announced |
| Months 2–3 | Old way removed; leaders asking in routines; first measured results |
| Months 4–6 | Built into the job (procedures, induction, goals); BAU owner running the measures; 180-day check |

Adjust to the measurement plan's review points and the size of the change.
