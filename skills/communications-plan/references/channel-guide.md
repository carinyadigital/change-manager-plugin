# Choosing channels

Original guidance written for this plugin. Read it when matching channels to
audiences and messages in the communications plan. Ask how each group
actually gets information before choosing — the right channel is the one
that group uses, not the one the project prefers.

## Channel × purpose

| Channel | Best for | Two-way? | Reaches frontline / shift / offline staff? | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Face-to-face from the manager (team meeting, huddle, one-to-one) | What it means for me; sensitive news; questions | Yes | Yes | Manager needs a pack and to be briefed first |
| Leader-led town hall or all-hands (in person or live online) | Why we are changing; direction; visible sponsorship | Partly (live Q&A) | Partly | One-off; record it; follow with manager conversations |
| Leader briefing session | Equipping managers before their teams hear | Yes | n/a | Leave time between briefing and team cascade |
| Email | Formal notice; dates; links; a record | No | Often not | Opens are not understanding; keep short |
| Intranet page or hub | The single source of truth; FAQ; job aids | No | Often not | Must be kept current; date every update |
| Team chat channel or post | Quick updates; reminders; Q&A threads | Yes | Depends on the group | Easy to miss; pin key posts |
| Printed notices, posters, screens in break rooms | Reminders and dates for sites without desk access | No | Yes | Lead time to print; remove when out of date |
| Shift-start briefing or toolbox talk | Frontline and shift teams | Yes | Yes | Script must be short and fit the briefing slot |
| SMS or app notification | Urgent, very short reminders | No | Yes | Consent and privacy rules; use sparingly |
| Video (short, recorded) | Demos; leader message that people can watch later | No | If devices allow | Captions; keep under three minutes |
| Q&A session or drop-in clinic | Detailed questions; worried groups | Yes | If scheduled across shifts | Publicise answers afterwards |
| Champions and super-users | Peer voice; local questions; feedback | Yes | Yes | Need a toolkit and an escalation route |
| Pulse survey | Checking understanding and sentiment | Yes (aggregate) | If accessible on site | Minimum group size; close the loop |
| FAQ (living document) | Repeated questions; consistency | Feed via inbox | If printed or on shared devices | Date it; mark "not yet decided" items |

## Rules of thumb

- **Higher impact → closer to the person.** A group with headline impact 3–4
  hears from its manager face to face first; email confirms.
- **Two channels for anything important**, at least one of them two-way.
- **Same core narrative everywhere**, adapted to what each group needs to
  know and do.
- **Sensitive news in person first** (role, location, hours, pay, job
  security), then written confirmation, then broadcast.
- **Plan a check** for each high-impact group — a huddle question, a pulse
  item or champion feedback — so you know the message landed.

## Phase rhythm (typical)

| Phase | Typical items | Rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before announcement | Leader briefings; manager packs; sensitive conversations | Once, in sequence |
| Announcement | Sponsor message; manager team conversations; FAQ v1; intranet hub | Same week |
| Design and build | "What's decided, what isn't" updates; engagement sessions; champion updates | Fortnightly |
| 6–2 weeks before go-live | What changes for you; training invitations; readiness reminders | Weekly |
| Final 2 weeks | Day-one guide; support route; cutover dates; what to do if stuck | Several times a week |
| Go-live and hypercare | Daily tips; known issues and fixes; "you said, we did" | Daily, then weekly |
| Sustain | Early results; recognition; old way switched off; where help lives now | Monthly |

The rhythm is a starting point. Adjust to the size of the change and how
saturated the groups are (`portfolio-saturation`).
