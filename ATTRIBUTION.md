## Bundling adapted material later

The plugin ships no third-party text, templates or instruments. If a future
release adapts openly licensed material, the adapted file must carry a header
stating the source, licence and what was changed, and be listed in this file.
Skills, references, templates and user-facing documents do not name, link to
or cite government bodies, public-sector publications or client
organisations.

See the release gate in [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).
