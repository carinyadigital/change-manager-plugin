# Kotter — cheat-sheet

Kotter's 8 Steps / 8 Accelerators belong to Kotter Inc. Described here in our
own words. Further reading: https://www.kotterinc.com/methodology/8-steps/

## The idea

A leader-led campaign, grouped in three movements:

| Movement | Steps (paraphrased) |
| --- | --- |
| Create the climate | Build a sense of urgency around a real opportunity; form a guiding group with authority and credibility; shape a clear vision and the initiatives that serve it |
| Engage and enable | Enlist a broad volunteer network; remove barriers that slow people down; generate visible early wins |
| Implement and sustain | Keep pushing after early wins; embed the change in how the organisation runs |

The later "accelerators" version treats these as running in parallel rather
than strictly in sequence.

## When it fits

Top-down, organisation-wide changes that need momentum and energy — strategy
shifts, transformations, culture change.
